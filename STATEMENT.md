## problem statement
Many people such as students and professionals whi often have busy schedules filled with classes, meetings, and events. Sometimes they accidentally get scheduling conflicts occur having events around the same timing, leading to missed activities or getting double booked. There is a need for a simple tool to check for and prevent scheduling conflicts to achieve personal time management and clarity.

## SCOPE
the project includes a Python CRUD application aimed at managing and organizing daily events, with a specific focus on detecting and preventing scheduling conflicts. The scope covers:
 
 view schedule, add events, update events, and delet events.
 
 Automatically checking for overlapping events on the same date and time.
 
 Providing a streamlined, user-friendly interface for efficient event management.
