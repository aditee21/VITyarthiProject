## ** PROBLEM STATEMENT **
For students and professionals who often manage their busy schedule filled with classes, meettings, and events. Accidental schedule conlicts occurs when two events overlap in timing, this leads to missed opportunities or getting double booked. There is a clear need for a simple tool to check for and prevent scheduling conflicts to improve personal time management and priorities.

## SCOPE OF THE PROJECT

This project provides a python CRUD application aimed at managing and organizing daily events with a essentially focusing on detecting and preventing scheduling conflicts. The scope covers:

    Viewing, adding, updating, and deleting events.
    
    Automatically checking for overlapping events on the same date and time.

    Providing a user-friendly interface hence, improving usability .

## TARGET USERS

Students that haveto managing classes, club activities, and exams.Professionals scheduling meetings or appointments. For anyone looking to organize events neatly and avoid conflicts and overlaps in their schedule.

## HIGH LEVEL FEATURES 

**Add events:** Users can input new events including fulll time and date.

**View Schedule:** Displays thecurrent schedule that already exists with full informaton from time to date .

**Delete Events:** User can remove that are no longer relevant.

**Conflict Detection:** Prevents addition or updating of events that would overlap with others, ensuring a conflict-free schedule.

**Workflow:** Neat user interaction and user friendly.
